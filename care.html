05 · Using it well

In service of people.

These tools help with preparation. The care, discernment, and presence are still yours.

Great fit

  • Drafting, outlining, and brainstorming
  • Organizing notes, tasks, and schedules
  • Reformatting or simplifying existing content
  • Building trackers, templates, and checklists
  • Preparing questions, options, and agendas

Pause and be careful

  • Private information. Do not paste names with prayer requests, counseling, health, finances, or children’s details. Use placeholders like “a member.”
  • Pastoral moments. Notes of care should sound like you. Brainstorm, then write it yourself.
  • Decisions about people. Hiring, discipline, and care need human judgment.
  • Facts and Scripture. Verify dates, quotes, and references.
  • Team policy. Follow current Life Church guidelines on approved tools and data.

A quick check before you send

Is it accurate? Does it sound like us? Would the person receiving it feel known and cared for? If not, edit until it does.
