04 · Your role

Find ideas in your own week.

Ideas stick when they come from real work. Try this alone or with your team.

  1. List your weekWrite down everything you did in the last five working days, even small things.
  2. Mark the drainsCircle tasks that are repetitive, start from scratch, or involve sorting information.
  3. Ask 3 questionsCould it draft this? Organize this? Check this?
  4. Test onePick the smallest item and try it this week. Keep the prompt if it works.
  5. Share itTell your team what worked so others can borrow it.

Or let the assistant interview you

I work at a church as a [your role]. Interview me, one question at a time, about what I do in a typical week: recurring tasks, deadlines, the work I put off, and the work I wish I had more time for. After 8 to 10 questions, suggest 10 specific ways an AI assistant could help me, ranked by time saved and ease of starting. Include a starter prompt for the top three. Do not suggest anything that requires sharing private information about people.
